Draft and archived — a badge and a filter, never a sort

Status is a qualifier, not an ordering. Sorting by it interleaves rows for a reason that has nothing to do with what is being chosen, and buries good products under bad ones. It belongs on the row and in the filter.

The runtime already blocks them — and that was a fixed bug. isSellable requires status === "active", under the comment “Logic-step §7 bug 2 — a DRAFT or ARCHIVED product carries a real price and used to pass this check, so it could reach a shopper.” New publishes bake status and enforce it; older bakes have no status field and keep their exact pre-fix behaviour. The picker is the one surface that never says so. catalogProducts ships id, title, imageUrl, price, description, tagKeys, collectionIds — no status. The builder index carries it (:286) and the publish bake carries it (quizPublish.ts:616); Step 1 is the gap. Drafts stay in the list, badged. That is the case worth protecting: building a quiz ahead of a launch and publishing the products later is a real thing merchants do, and hiding drafts makes it impossible. The runtime treats them identically; the merchant’s intent is opposite, so the UI should not.

Collections have no equivalent, and that is not an oversight. Shopify collections are not draft/active — they are published to sales channels, which this app does not store.
