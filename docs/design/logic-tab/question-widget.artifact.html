The question widget

The chosen layout, on its own. This is the questions widget only — the STYLE header above it and the Rules widget below it are unchanged and are not part of this update. Everything here is live: pick a question in the rail, change its role, click a cell to map it, drop a card from the tray, remove one mapping at a time.

What this widget does

  • Picks results and Narrows map to different things, so they get different pickers. On the deciding question an answer points at a step-1 recommendation; on a narrowing question it points at a catalog value. Both open the same shape of control — search, category chips, tick several, Done — because the act is the same even though the pool is not.
  • The tray is the step-1 output, as cards. A thumbnail and a name, sized to click without aiming. Clicking one opens what is inside it; clicking it again arms it, and then any answer cell will take it.
  • The tray fills two rows and then stops. How many cards that is cannot be a fixed number — names run 67px to 175px and the pane is fluid — so the widget measures after layout and hides whatever lands on a third row, handing the remainder to See N more. Tick the box above to load 200 recommendations and watch it hold.
  • An answer can hold more than one mapping, and each one leaves on its own. Every mapping is a chip with its own ×. Placing from the tray adds; it never silently replaces what is already there.
  • The cell is the button. There is no + to find — clicking the white space of a cell opens its picker. The chips and their ×s stop the click, so the three targets inside one cell stay distinct.
  • Ticking stages, Done writes. Nothing changes in the row until you commit, and Cancel restores what was there — a Cancel that keeps your edits is just a second Done.
  • A mapped value keeps the field it came from. "dry" alone reads as a tag when it is really the skin_type metafield. It could never mis-route the engine — each kind is stored in its own array and matched against different product data — but a label implying the wrong kind sends a merchant looking in the wrong place in Shopify.
  • The cell you changed flashes briefly, so when a picker closes your eye lands on what moved.
  • One explainer, at the top. How this works holds the three role definitions instead of repeating a line under every question.

Not in scope

  • The STYLE header (Attributes + Rules, Check my logic, Switch to rules only) and the Rules widget below the table are unchanged. Nothing in this update touches them.
  • The ✦ Map AI-fill button was removed from this widget. The undo affordance it used still exists in the code and is dormant.
